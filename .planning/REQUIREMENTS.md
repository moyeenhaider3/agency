# Requirements: AgenticeEra Solution

**Defined:** 2026-11-08
**Core Value:** Show business owners exactly how much revenue, cash, and staff time they can recover by replacing repetitive manual work with always-on operational support.

## v1 Requirements

### Proposition and Navigation

- [ ] **PROP-01**: Visitor can identify AgenticeEra Solution, its New Delhi/global service footprint, and its outcome-led promise in the first viewport.
- [ ] **PROP-02**: Visitor can navigate to industry results, the operating model, ROI calculator, pricing, and FAQs from the primary navigation.
- [ ] **PROP-03**: Visitor can switch between India/INR and Global/USD views and see the relevant pricing and financial figures update consistently.

### Conversion

- [ ] **CONV-01**: Visitor can open a 20-second live-call demo modal, provide name, industry, and phone number, and receive clear submission feedback.
- [ ] **CONV-02**: Visitor can submit a 15-minute revenue-audit enquiry with name, company, WhatsApp number, and primary bottleneck.
- [ ] **CONV-03**: Visitor can access a persistent mobile/desktop conversion action for the live demo and pilot/audit path.

### Industry Outcomes

- [ ] **IND-01**: Visitor can switch among five industry playbooks: real estate, healthcare, D2C/e-commerce, CA/manufacturing/wholesale, and global B2B SaaS/staffing/consulting.
- [ ] **IND-02**: Each industry playbook displays its costly problem, day-one operational change, and supplied financial impact.
- [ ] **IND-03**: Visitor can compare manual operating costs with the 24/7 digital-team model using the supplied 20-second, 90/10, and payback proof points.

### ROI and Pricing

- [ ] **ROI-01**: Visitor can select an industry and adjust a relevant monthly volume and current staff/ad-spend/deal-value input.
- [ ] **ROI-02**: Calculator displays estimated monthly savings or recovered revenue, eliminated manual hours, and estimated payback period in the selected currency.
- [ ] **PRICE-01**: Visitor can compare Starter, Growth Department Fleet, and Complete Digital Operations Suite plans with setup fee, recurring price, deliverables, expected value, and payback.
- [ ] **PRICE-02**: Visitor can understand the zero-markup usage promise and the 14-day founding-partner pilot/guarantee without hidden pricing language.

### Trust and Explanation

- [ ] **TRUST-01**: Visitor can understand the three-step workflow audit, watch-mode pilot, and one-click human-control model.
- [ ] **TRUST-02**: Visitor can find plain-language assurances covering data ownership, privacy, India data residency, opt-out handling, and critical-response reliability.
- [ ] **TRUST-03**: Visitor can expand accessible FAQ items addressing voice quality, existing software compatibility, risky requests, and the pilot guarantee.
- [ ] **TRUST-04**: Visitor can distinguish illustrative examples and estimates from confirmed guarantees or independently verified results.

### Quality

- [ ] **QUAL-01**: All primary sections and interactions work responsively on mobile, tablet, and desktop.
- [ ] **QUAL-02**: Keyboard users can operate navigation, tabs, sliders, modal, FAQ, and forms with visible focus and appropriate labels.
- [ ] **QUAL-03**: Visitor-facing copy avoids the banned technical jargon and consistently prioritizes business outcomes.
- [ ] **QUAL-04**: The page uses the specified alabaster/white, obsidian, emerald, and amber visual system with restrained motion and readable financial numerals.

## v2 Requirements

### Production Integrations

- **INTG-01**: Live voice demo requests trigger real outbound calls with approved scripts and Hindi/English/Hinglish handling.
- **INTG-02**: Lead and audit forms submit to a production CRM, calendar, analytics, and notification workflow.
- **INTG-03**: Operational departments connect to WhatsApp, CRM, Shopify, Tally/Zoho, and other customer systems.

### Customer Operations

- **OPS-01**: Customer can access a secure portal with department activity, approvals, and executive summaries.
- **OPS-02**: Customer can review, approve, edit, or reject high-stakes actions from a controlled workflow.

## Out of Scope

| Feature | Reason |
|---------|--------|
| Authenticated customer dashboard | Not needed to validate the public marketing and conversion experience |
| Real outbound calling or messaging in v1 | Requires production providers, consent, monitoring, and compliance workflows |
| CRM, calendar, Shopify, Tally, or Zoho integrations in v1 | Deferred until conversion demand and integration requirements are validated |
| User accounts and billing | Outside the initial public-site milestone |
| Unsubstantiated case studies or performance claims | Conflicts with the trust-first positioning |
