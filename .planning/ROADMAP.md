# Roadmap: AgenticeEra Solution

## Overview

Deliver the marketing frontend as a sequence of vertical slices: first establish the outcome-led proposition and trust model, then make the business case tangible through industry playbooks, ROI, and pricing, and finally connect the conversion paths with responsive, accessible, production-ready interaction polish.

## Phases

- [x] **Phase 1: Executive Proposition & Trust** - Make the promise, operating model, and assurances clear at first glance.
- [ ] **Phase 2: Industry Outcomes, ROI & Pricing** - Let visitors explore relevant business impact and payback.
- [ ] **Phase 3: Conversion & Experience Readiness** - Turn understanding into enquiries through accessible, responsive conversion flows.

## Phase Details

### Phase 1: Executive Proposition & Trust
**Goal**: Visitors understand what AgenticeEra Solution does, how the 90/10 operating model works, and why the service is credible enough to explore.
**Depends on**: Nothing (first phase)
**Requirements**: PROP-01, PROP-02, IND-03, TRUST-01, TRUST-02, TRUST-04, QUAL-03, QUAL-04
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. Visitor can identify the AgenticeEra brand, New Delhi/global footprint, and outcome-led promise in the first viewport.
  2. Visitor can navigate to industry results, operating model, ROI calculator, pricing, and FAQs from the primary navigation.
  3. Visitor can compare manual operations with the 24/7 digital-team model using the supplied 20-second, 90/10, and payback proof points.
  4. Visitor can understand the audit, watch-mode pilot, human-control model, data/privacy assurances, and which examples are illustrative.
  5. Visitor encounters plain business language and the specified visual system without unsupported claims or technical jargon.

### Phase 2: Industry Outcomes, ROI & Pricing
**Goal**: Visitors can select a relevant business scenario, quantify its likely value, and compare a transparent path to starting.
**Depends on**: Phase 1
**Requirements**: PROP-03, IND-01, IND-02, ROI-01, ROI-02, PRICE-01, PRICE-02
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. Visitor can switch among all five industry playbooks and see each industry's costly problem, day-one change, and supplied financial impact.
  2. Visitor can switch between India/INR and Global/USD views while keeping industry and page context.
  3. Visitor can adjust an industry-relevant volume and current input, then see estimated savings or recovered revenue, eliminated hours, and payback in the selected currency.
  4. Visitor can compare Starter, Growth Department Fleet, and Complete Digital Operations Suite plans with setup, recurring price, deliverables, value, and payback.
  5. Visitor can understand the zero-markup usage promise and 14-day founding-partner pilot/guarantee without hidden pricing language.

### Phase 3: Conversion & Experience Readiness
**Goal**: Visitors can act on the business case through clear enquiry flows that work reliably across devices and input methods.
**Depends on**: Phase 2
**Requirements**: CONV-01, CONV-02, CONV-03, TRUST-03, QUAL-01, QUAL-02
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. Visitor can open the 20-second live-call demo modal, submit name, industry, and phone number, and receive clear feedback without requiring a real outbound call.
  2. Visitor can submit a 15-minute revenue-audit enquiry with name, company, WhatsApp number, and primary bottleneck.
  3. Visitor can reach the live-demo and pilot/audit actions through persistent mobile and desktop conversion controls.
  4. Visitor can expand accessible FAQ items covering voice quality, existing software, risky requests, and the pilot guarantee.
  5. The complete experience works responsively on mobile, tablet, and desktop, and keyboard users can operate navigation, tabs, sliders, modal, FAQ, and forms with visible focus and labels.
